## Basic Movements

### Wave Hello
**Prompt**: `"Make the character wave hello"`

**What Happens**: Character raises right arm and waves hand side to side in a friendly greeting gesture.

**Variations**:
- `"Wave hello enthusiastically"` - More energetic waving
- `"Wave goodbye slowly"` - Sadder, slower wave
- `"Wave with both hands"` - Both arms waving

---

### Jump
**Prompt**: `"Make the character jump"`

**What Happens**: Character crouches down (anticipation), springs up into the air, and lands softly with bent knees.

**Variations**:
- `"Do a small hop"` - Quick, minimal jump
- `"Jump really high"` - Exaggerated jump with full extension
- `"Jump forward"` - Jump with horizontal movement

---

### Walk
**Prompt**: `"Have the character walk forward"`

**What Happens**: Character performs a walking cycle with alternating legs and swinging arms.

**Variations**:
- `"Walk slowly and tiredly"` - Slumped posture, dragging feet
- `"Walk confidently"` - Upright posture, purposeful stride
- `"Sneak forward carefully"` - Crouched, slow, deliberate steps

---

### Reach
**Prompt**: `"Make them reach for something high"`

**What Happens**: Character extends arm upward, standing on tiptoes, stretching toward something overhead.

**Variations**:
- `"Reach down to pick something up"` - Bend down, grab motion
- `"Reach out to grab something"` - Extend arm forward
- `"Stretch arms overhead"` - Both arms up, stretching

---

## Complex Actions

### Backflip
**Prompt**: `"Do a backflip"`

**What Happens**: Character crouches, launches backward, rotates 360° in the air, and lands on feet.

**Variations**:
- `"Do a front flip"` - Forward rotation
- `"Do a side flip"` - Cartwheel-style rotation
- `"Do a backflip and land in a pose"` - Ends in dramatic stance

---

### Run and Jump
**Prompt**: `"Run forward and jump over something"`

**What Happens**: Running buildup, powerful takeoff, arc through air, landing with forward momentum.

**Variations**:
- `"Run and dive"` - Horizontal dive motion
- `"Sprint and leap"` - Maximum speed and distance
- `"Jog and hop over obstacle"` - Casual, low jump

---

### Fall
**Prompt**: `"Fall dramatically to the ground"`

**What Happens**: Character loses balance, flails arms, falls backward, and lands on ground.

**Variations**:
- `"Trip and fall forward"` - Stumble and catch self
- `"Collapse from exhaustion"` - Slow, tired fall
- `"Get knocked back"` - Sudden backward impact

---

## Emotional Expressions

### Victory Dance
**Prompt**: `"Celebrate with a victory dance"`

**What Happens**: Arms pump in the air, hips swivel, energetic celebration with bouncing.

**Variations**:
- `"Do a subtle victory fist pump"` - Contained celebration
- `"Go wild with excitement"` - Maximum energy celebration
- `"Victory pose with arms raised"` - Static heroic pose

---

### Disappointment
**Prompt**: `"Slump shoulders in disappointment"`

**What Happens**: Shoulders drop, head hangs down, arms fall to sides, defeated posture.

**Variations**:
- `"Hang head in shame"` - More pronounced slouch
- `"Shrug in confusion"` - Shoulders up, arms out
- `"Sigh heavily"` - Body movement for deep breath

---

### Excitement
**Prompt**: `"Jump excitedly with arms raised"`

**What Happens**: Multiple bouncing jumps, arms waving overhead, energetic movement.

**Variations**:
- `"Bounce with anticipation"` - Smaller, rapid bounces
- `"Clap hands excitedly"` - Hands coming together repeatedly
- `"Skip happily"` - Alternating skip steps

---

### Anger
**Prompt**: `"Shake fist angrily"`

**What Happens**: Raise fist, shake it threateningly, tense posture, possible stamping.

**Variations**:
- `"Stomp foot in frustration"` - Foot impact, bent posture
- `"Cross arms angrily"` - Defensive, closed-off stance
- `"Point accusingly"` - Aggressive pointing gesture

---

## Sports & Activities

### Throw
**Prompt**: `"Wind up and throw a ball"`

**What Happens**: Arm winds back, body rotates, powerful forward throw motion, follow-through.

**Variations**:
- `"Throw underhand"` - Gentle, upward toss
- `"Pitch like a baseball player"` - Full windup and pitch
- `"Toss something casually"` - Relaxed, one-handed toss

---

### Kick
**Prompt**: `"Kick a soccer ball"`

**What Happens**: Wind up leg back, powerful forward kick, balance on standing leg, follow-through.

**Variations**:
- `"Kick high like martial arts"` - High roundhouse kick
- `"Punt a football"` - Drop and kick motion
- `"Kick something away angrily"` - Aggressive kicking motion

---

### Swing
**Prompt**: `"Swing a baseball bat"`

**What Happens**: Bat back in ready position, powerful swing through, follow-through rotation.

**Variations**:
- `"Swing a golf club"` - Full backswing and follow-through
- `"Swing a sword"` - Overhead or side slash
- `"Swing on a rope"` - Pendulum swinging motion

---

### Climb
**Prompt**: `"Climb up a ladder"`

**What Happens**: Alternating hand-over-hand pulling, legs stepping up, vertical movement.

**Variations**:
- `"Pull yourself up"` - Single pull-up motion
- `"Climb a rope"` - Hand-over-hand rope climbing
- `"Scramble up a wall"` - Desperate climbing motion

---

## Dance Moves

### Moonwalk
**Prompt**: `"Do the moonwalk"`

**What Happens**: Sliding backward while appearing to walk forward, smooth gliding motion.

**Variations**:
- `"Slide to the side smoothly"` - Lateral sliding
- `"Glide backward mysteriously"` - Floating backward movement
- `"Shuffle dance"` - Quick foot shuffling

---

### Spin
**Prompt**: `"Spin around gracefully"`

**What Happens**: Arms out, body rotates 360° smoothly, graceful movement.

**Variations**:
- `"Spin rapidly like a top"` - Fast, multiple rotations
- `"Twirl on one foot"` - Ballet-style pirouette
- `"Do a breakdance spin"` - Dynamic floor spin

---

### Headbang
**Prompt**: `"Headbang to music"`

**What Happens**: Head and upper body rock forward and back rapidly, energetic motion.

**Variations**:
- `"Nod head to a beat"` - Gentle rhythmic nodding
- `"Rock out with air guitar"` - Full body rock motion
- `"Pump fist to music"` - Arm pumping rhythm

---

## Combat & Action

### Punch
**Prompt**: `"Throw a powerful punch"`

**What Happens**: Wind up, rotate hips, extend arm forward explosively, recoil back.

**Variations**:
- `"Do a one-two punch combo"` - Quick jab, cross sequence
- `"Uppercut"` - Upward punch from below
- `"Haymaker swing"` - Wide, powerful hook

---

### Block
**Prompt**: `"Block an incoming attack"`

**What Happens**: Arms come up defensively, slight lean back, protective stance.

**Variations**:
- `"Dodge to the side"` - Quick lateral movement
- `"Duck under something"` - Crouch dodge
- `"Parry and counter"` - Deflect and strike back

---

### Slash
**Prompt**: `"Swing a sword in a wide arc"`

**What Happens**: Weapon back, powerful sideways slash, full body rotation, follow-through.

**Variations**:
- `"Overhead sword slam"` - Vertical downward strike
- `"Quick sword jab"` - Forward thrust motion
- `"Spinning sword attack"` - 360° rotating slash

---

## Tips for Better Prompts

### Be Specific About Emotion
- ❌ `"Make them move"`
- ✅ `"Walk tiredly with slumped shoulders"`

### Describe the Intent
- ❌ `"Move arm"`
- ✅ `"Reach up excitedly to grab something"`

### Include Context
- ❌ `"Jump"`
- ✅ `"Jump back in surprise"`

### Specify Speed/Style
- ❌ `"Wave"`
- ✅ `"Wave hello enthusiastically with big arm movements"`

### Mention Key Poses
- ❌ `"Fight move"`
- ✅ `"Wind up and throw a powerful punch, then return to guard"`

### Combine Actions
- ❌ `"Dance"`
- ✅ `"Do a victory dance with jumping, arm pumping, and spinning"`

### Use Animation Principles
- **Anticipation**: `"Crouch down then jump high"`
- **Follow-through**: `"Swing bat with full follow-through"`
- **Exaggeration**: `"Fall dramatically and exaggeratedly"`
- **Arcs**: `"Swing arm in a wide circular arc"`

## Common Prompt Patterns

### Pattern 1: Action + Emotion
```
"[Action] [emotion/adverb]"
Examples:
- "Walk sadly"
- "Jump excitedly"
- "Wave enthusiastically"
```

### Pattern 2: Preparation + Action + Recovery
```
"[Wind up], [main action], [follow through]"
Examples:
- "Crouch down, jump high, land softly"
- "Pull back, throw hard, follow through"
- "Step back, kick forward, return to stance"
```

### Pattern 3: Sequential Actions
```
"[Action 1] then [action 2]"
Examples:
- "Run forward then jump"
- "Wave hello then bow"
- "Punch then block"
```

### Pattern 4: Style Specification
```
"[Action] like [style reference]"
Examples:
- "Jump like a superhero"
- "Walk like a zombie"
- "Dance like celebrating a victory"
```

## Experiment and Iterate!

The LLM understands natural language, so don't be afraid to:
- Try unusual combinations
- Ask for specific details
- Reference real-world actions
- Describe feelings and intentions
- Request multiple variations

**Remember**: The more descriptive and specific your prompt, the better the animation will match your vision!
